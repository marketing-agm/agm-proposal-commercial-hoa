Typography

H1 — Page title (Source Serif 4, 28px)

H2 — Section header (serif, 18px)

H3 — Card title (serif, 16px)

Body text — DM Sans at the app's 14px base, line height 1.5. Headings are serif, UI/body is sans; that contrast is the brand. (Bump body to 16px on owner/tenant pages.)

Small / muted text — captions, labels, footnotes.

Buttons

Primary is near-black, not blue — blue is reserved for links, focus, and AI affordances. Tab to a button to see the accent focus ring.

Form Inputs

Enter the property as it appears in AppFolio.
Enter a valid month, e.g. 05/2026.

Click into a field to see the signature focus ring: border-color: var(--accent) + box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px var(--accent-light).
